## Decisions confirmed by exploration

- Source completion can precede job creation without allowing premature credit.
- Policy rollover cannot change the version pinned to a committed judgment.
- Retry exhaustion and credit rejection are distinct terminal outcomes.
- Rejected judgments cannot award credits.
- Cumulative page versions processed in either order preserve page and student ledger totals without double award.
- Interleaved accepted sources cannot raise a student's modeled earning-day total above `DailyCap`.
- A completed job always has a visible terminal judgment and committed ledger decision.
- The normal pipeline state never regresses because of credit transitions.
